Strike Monkey is a 5x4, 1,024 Ways slot set in a retro bowling alley. Bowler Wilds collect the values of every Pin on the board, adding instant awards to any Ways win. Three to five Golden Ball Scatters award 10-15 Free Spins. During the feature, collecting Bowlers fills a three-stage meter; each stage grants 10 extra spins and turns another low symbol into Pins on later spins. Players can buy Spare Round or Perfect Game. RTP is 96%, with a maximum win of 10,000x the bet.
