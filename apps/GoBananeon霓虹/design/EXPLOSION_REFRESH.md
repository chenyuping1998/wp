# Neon blast presentation

The blast keeps the game's pink/cyan plasma identity while separating anticipation, destructive impact, vapour and result reveal. The board scoring and scatter survival rules are unchanged.

- `src/game/blastTimeline.ts` owns the timing for 1–5 covered reels. Charge stays at 380 ms to match `BlastSwell`, the frame bow and fuse audio. The whole presentation completes in approximately 1.69 s for one reel and 2.15 s for five reels.
- `ReelBlast.svelte` adds converging charge rings, a compressed first hit, three delayed irregular pressure fronts and a restrained full-board exposure lift. It masks the board with opaque per-cell apertures until each replacement arrives, then sweeps each aperture open from top to bottom. A Scatter never enters the shard or refill lists.
- `BlastFx.svelte` uses real symbol art for the fragments, a short bloom and horizontal flare for heat, drifting smoke lobes for volume, and normal-blended replacement symbols with small additive trails. `blastFragments.ts` first finds the visible alpha bounds, makes up to nine uneven cuts, trims each cut again and discards empty pieces. This preserves readable fragments for the transparent V4 symbols and caps full-board real-art debris at 360 sprites including edge copies. Large pieces travel and turn more slowly than fine pieces, with a shared outward/upward impulse.
- Smoke emission is capped at 10 emitters / 60 lobes, sharing a single 128×128 texture generated by `blastTextures.ts`; no blur filters or board-sized render targets are added. Normal and additive particles occupy separate contiguous containers, avoiding a blend-mode batch flush for every individual body/edge pair.
- The visual clock and awaited game-flow promise share `blastEndAt`. The next win evaluation cannot begin while a trailing column flash remains. Clear, restart and component destruction invalidate pending board writes; all timers and temporary textures are cleaned up.

## Audio event clock

All times are milliseconds from receiving `reelBlast`.

| Cue | 1 reel | 5 reels |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Fuse | 0 | 0 |
| Detonate + shatter + frame impact | 380 | 380 |
| Smoke / pillar expansion | 554 | 660 |
| Hidden board swap | 690 | 880 |
| Reveal | 810 | 1060 |
| Final column starts its settling flash | 1407 | 1867 |
| End | 1687 | 2147 |

`soundBlastDetonate` includes `width` so audio can grow across intermediate widths. `soundBlastColumn` stays one cue per reel instead of playing on every cell.
