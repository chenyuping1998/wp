are real human performances. Pickle boss and spicy chef use different excerpts
and takes with ordinary varispeed, EQ, gain, fades and stereo decorrelation.
The chef is a processed character voice, not a claim of a female performer.
No AI voice synthesis, cloning, impersonation or identity modelling is used.
