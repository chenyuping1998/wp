Base Game

Static Shift is a 5x4 video slot with 1,024 ways. Matching symbols on three or more adjacent reels pay from the leftmost reel. Relays are Wilds on reels 2 to 5. Signal Packs carry values of 1x to 50x the base bet and do not form ways.

Signal Relay

When Relays and Signal Packs land together, each Relay collects the total value of every Pack once. Collection wins are added to ways wins.

Free Spins

Three, four or five Tape Deck Scatters award 10, 12 or 15 spins. Each Relay advances a meter, even without Packs. Reaching 4, 8 and 12 Relays adds 10 spins and raises subsequent collection multipliers to x2, x3 and x10. Packs can reach 250x. Scatters do not appear during Free Spins.

Buy Bonus

Free Spins cost 100x the base bet. Super Free Spins cost 150x, starting with 10 spins, four Relays counted and x2 collections.

RTP & Max Win

RTP is 96.05% for base play, 96.07% for Free Spins and 96.09% for Super Free Spins, a 0.04% spread. Maximum win is 10,000x the base bet in every mode.
