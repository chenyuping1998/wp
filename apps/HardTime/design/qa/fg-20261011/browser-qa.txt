THE SEARCHLIGHT

A Searchlight lights its reel from where it lands down to the bottom, turning those positions Wild and giving each one a multiplier of 1× to 100×. Any payline crossing lit positions adds together every multiplier above 1×.

OVERLAPPING BEAMS

When a Searchlight lands on a reel that is already lit, every position the two beams share has its multiplier doubled, up to 100×. Positions the new beam reaches for the first time take its own value.
