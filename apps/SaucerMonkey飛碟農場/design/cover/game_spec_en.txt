Saucer Monkey is a 5x4, 1,024 Ways slot set on a moonlit 1950s cornfield. Captain Wilds collect the values of every Cow on the board, adding instant awards to any Ways win. Three to five Golden Saucer Scatters award 10-15 Free Spins. During the feature, collecting Captains fills a three-stage meter; each stage grants 10 extra spins and turns another low symbol into Cows on later spins. Players can buy Close Encounter or Mothership. RTP is 96%, with a maximum win of 10,000x the bet.
